Weighted demand in catchment
Sum of demand point weights within 3km. The core numerator of opportunity. Uses Haversine distance from candidate lat/lng to each demand point. — Why it matters: Directly measures how many customers are reachable.
D = Σ weight_i for all i where haversine(cand, D_i) ≤ 3km
